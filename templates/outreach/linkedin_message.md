# LinkedIn message (after connection accepted)

Draft only; the candidate sends. Target 60 to 110 words. One ask.

## Guidance

- Open with the thread you already share: the note they accepted, or the role you applied to.
- Two sentences of proof, from bullet ids. Pick the bullets closest to their team's work.
- One specific ask: a 15-minute call, a referral, or "is the team still hiring for this".
- Give them an easy out: "no worries if not".
- Link only to something concrete (GitHub, app page). No résumé attachment unless asked.
- Nothing from `config/qa.yaml: banned_phrases`. Max 2 em dashes.

## Skeleton

```
Hi <First>,

Thanks for connecting. <Why I'm writing: role / team, one line.>

<Proof sentence 1 (bullet id).> <Proof sentence 2 (bullet id).>

<Single ask.> No worries if not.

<Candidate first name>
```
