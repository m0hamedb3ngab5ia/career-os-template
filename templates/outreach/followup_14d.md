# Outreach follow-up, day 14 (final)

Same thread. Under 50 words. After this, mark the contact `no_reply` and stop.

## Guidance

- Close the loop politely. No guilt, no "last attempt".
- Leave the door open with a single line.
- No new ask beyond "reply if timing changes".

## Skeleton

```
Hi <First>,

<Acknowledge timing.> <Door left open, one line.>

<Candidate first name>
```
