# After applying: outreach to a recruiter or hiring manager

Send same day to 3 days after applying. About 100 words. **Not a mini cover letter:** one hook, one or two proof points.
LinkedIn variant: the same idea cut roughly in half (connection note <= 300 characters).

## Skeleton

```
Subject: [ROLE] application

Hi [NAME],

I just applied for the [ROLE] position at [COMPANY] and wanted to reach out directly.

What caught my attention was [SPECIFIC CONNECTION: one sentence specific to the company or role].
[MOST RELEVANT EXPERIENCE, one sentence.] [SECOND RELEVANT SIGNAL, only if useful.]

[One sentence on why the candidate is reaching out rather than waiting.]

I've attached my resume in case it's helpful. I'd be glad to talk through any of my work.

Best,
<Candidate first name>
```

## Example

EXAMPLE (fictional candidate Alex Example).

```
Subject: Backend SWE application

Hi Dan,

I just applied for the Backend SWE (Payments) position at Acme and wanted to reach out directly.

What caught my attention was the ledger team's work on settlement retries. At Initech, I built a FastAPI service that ingests Kafka order events into PostgreSQL, 2 million a day.

The role looks close to the engineering I want to keep doing, so I wanted to introduce myself rather than stay a name in the queue.

I've attached my resume in case it's helpful. I'd be glad to talk through any of my work.

Best,
Alex
```
