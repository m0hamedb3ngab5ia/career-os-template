# After rejection (optional): keep the relationship

Only when the candidate wants to stay in touch with the company. Manual. 40 to 70 words. No arguing the decision,
no asking them to reconsider.

## Skeleton

```
Subject: Re: [ROLE] position

Hi [NAME],

Thank you for letting me know, and for the time you and the team spent with me. I enjoyed learning about
[SPECIFIC TEAM/WORK] and would be glad to stay in touch for future roles on the team.

Best,
<Candidate first name>
```
