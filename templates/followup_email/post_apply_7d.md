# Follow-up email, 7 days after applying (no response)

Sent to a known contact at the company (Contacts tab) or the recruiter named in the posting. Never to
a generic `jobs@` inbox. Gmail auto-send only after the candidate confirms the template (see TODO.md).
50 to 90 words. Subject under 60 characters.

## Guidance

- State the role, the date applied, and the ATS confirmation if you have it.
- One line of proof tied to the top requirement in the posting (bullet id).
- Ask: whether the role is still open, or who owns the hiring for it.
- No "just following up", no apology, no résumé re-attach unless asked.
- Nothing from `config/qa.yaml: banned_phrases`.

## Skeleton

```
Subject: <Role> application, <Mon D>

Hi <First>,

I applied to <Role> on <date> (<ATS ref if any>). <One proof line, bullet id.>

Is the role still open? <Optional: who owns hiring for it.>

<Candidate first name>
<identity.phone> | <identity.linkedin>
```
