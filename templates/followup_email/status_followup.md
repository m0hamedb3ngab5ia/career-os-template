# No response: status follow-up

Send 7 to 14 days after an interview or recruiter conversation. 40 to 70 words. **Very short.** Do not resell the
candidate unless there is genuinely new information (a launch, an award, a promotion); then one sentence.

## Skeleton

```
Subject: Re: [ROLE] position

Hi [NAME],

I wanted to follow up on the [ROLE] position. I enjoyed speaking with [you / the team] and remain very interested in the
opportunity, especially after learning more about [SPECIFIC TEAM/WORK].

I know these processes can take time, but I wanted to check whether there have been any updates on next steps.

Thanks again,
<Candidate first name>
```
