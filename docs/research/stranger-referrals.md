# Why a stranger refers you (research, 2026-11-18)

Question: a random employee has no reason to help; what turns "stranger" into "I'll refer you"?

## Findings (evidence grade in brackets)
1. Askers underestimate yes: strangers agreed ~2-3x more than askers predicted; saying no has a social cost
   the asker forgets. [A: Flynn & Lake 2008, JPSP 95(1); Bohns et al. 2022, Psych Science]
   https://ecommons.cornell.edu/bitstream/handle/1813/75652/Bohns14_If_You_Need_Help_Just_Ask.pdf
2. The referrer's real cost is reputation, not effort: perceived risk lowers referring; vouching for
   someone they barely know is the riskiest case. [B: referral-risk studies; Sociological Science v5]
   https://sociologicalscience.com/download/vol-5/june/SocSci_v5_380to401.pdf
3. Bonus is a weak motive (11-40% say money drives referrals) and mentioning it can cut credibility.
   [B: LinkedIn internal survey; Applied Psychology, apps.12100]
   https://business.linkedin.com/talent-solutions/blog/2015/01/building-an-employee-referral-program-3-things-you-should-know
4. Shared traits raise compliance, even small ones, if real and uncommon (birthday, name, hometown);
   common ones do nothing. Same school/employer/niche path is the strong version. [A: Burger et al. 2004, PSPB]
   https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0956797610383436
5. One named recipient beats a group: individual emails got more, longer, more helpful replies.
   [A: Barron & Yechiam 2002, Computers in Human Behavior 18(5)]
6. A first ask for a short chat converts better than a first ask for a referral; make the referral
   effortless (req link, résumé attached, a paste-ready blurb, easy out). [C: practitioner blogs]
   https://careery.pro/blog/networking/how-to-get-referred-for-a-job

## Rules for drafts
- Ask "why would THIS person refer me?" and answer it in one clause.
- Shared background (school, ex-employer, city, niche path) → lead with it; it is the reason.
- None → de-risk them instead: one concrete proof they can check (shipped X, link), and offer a
  10-minute chat so they judge before vouching. Never mention the bonus.
- Personality words (go-getter, charismatic, witty) are claims; show one, never state them.
- Keep the easy out ("Totally understand if not").
