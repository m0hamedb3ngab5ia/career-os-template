# Résumé feedback

(short and general, no employer secrets). Optional: delete the file for no extra rules. Example content below.

## Bullets
- Say what the role was, what I contributed and why it mattered, not a list of tasks.

## Entries
- Give every experience entry at least two bullets, or cut it.
